# Product principles

> Answers: What does the product stand for, and how do we make trade-offs?
> Reviewed: 2026-10-08

What we value when two good things compete. Design principles (how screens should feel) are in `design-system/foundations/principles.md`.

<!-- replace: three to five, numbered PP-01, PP-02… The examples describe Helpline, a made-up help desk for small support teams. -->

### PP-01 Agents first (example)

We build for the person answering requests, not for reports about them. When an agent's speed and a manager's dashboard compete, the agent wins.

### PP-02 No customer waits unseen (example)

Nothing we ship may hide an unanswered request.

### PP-03 Fewer things, done well (example)

We'd rather do one job completely than three halfway.
