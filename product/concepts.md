# Concepts

> Answers: What are the core things in our domain, and what do we call them?
> Reviewed: 2026-10-08

The things the product is about and how they relate. How to word them on screen is in `design-system/foundations/writing.md`.

<!-- replace -->

| Concept | What it is | Not to be confused with |
|---|---|---|
| (example) Request | One question or problem from a customer, with its whole conversation | "Ticket" — we don't use that word |
| (example) Assignee | The one agent responsible for a request right now | Followers, who only get notified |
| (example) Priority | High, medium or low. Set by a person (see PD-02) | Status |
| (example) Status | New, in progress or done | Priority |
