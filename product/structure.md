# Structure

> Answers: What places does the product have, and how do people move between them?
> Reviewed: 2026-10-12

The map of this product: the places people go, what each one shows, and how they get from one to the next. It's the only part of the five planes that usually has no doc anywhere else, so it lives here.

A place is where someone goes to do part of a job, like a list, one item or a settings area. Name what it shows with the words in `concepts.md`. How screens are laid out in any product is in `design-system/patterns/GUIDE.md`, and what the approved screens look like is in `design-system/screens/`.

## When you change it

Add a place when a design that adds one is approved, not when it's first proposed. A PRD that needs a place that isn't here, or calls one by another name, gets logged by `frame`.
