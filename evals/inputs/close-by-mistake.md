# Brief: requests closed by mistake

**Screen:** the inbox, as it is today. A copy is in `work/prd/current-inbox.html`.

**What's wrong:** the Close button sits in the toolbar next to Assign and closes every selected request at once, with no warning. Last week two support tickets came from customers whose requests were closed by mistake and never answered.

**Ask:** make it hard to close requests by mistake, without slowing down agents who close many requests a day on purpose.
