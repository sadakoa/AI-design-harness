# Notes: team lead interviews, October 2026

From Kenji (UX research). Six interviews with team leads, 2026-10-05 to 2026-10-09. Recordings and transcripts: Research drive, folder "2026-10 team leads".

- 5 of 6 leads check the queue first thing in the morning and again after lunch. They don't watch it all day.
- 4 of 6 said the hardest part is knowing who is overloaded, not who is free.
- All 6 rebalance by reassigning requests, 3 to 10 times a day.
- 2 leads run teams of 25 or more agents; the other 4 run teams of 4 to 9.
- 3 of 6 export the queue to a spreadsheet every week to report to their own manager.
- 1 lead said agents hide hard requests by leaving them unassigned until the end of the day.
