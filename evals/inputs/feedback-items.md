# Things that came up this month

1. Support ticket review, October: 31% of customer follow-ups are "any update?" messages. (Source: support lead's ticket report.)
2. Two agents said they lose their place: after opening a request from the inbox and going back, the list jumps to the top. Seen on the inbox and on search results.
3. Agents call a hand-off a "transfer", PRDs say "hand-off", and the button on the request screen says "Reassign".
4. Review of the inbox and the request screen: the High priority badge uses the same red as error messages. Agents read High as "something broke".
5. Review of the request screen: "Assign" and "Close" look the same, and agents click Close by mistake. Two support tickets about requests closed by accident.
6. Sales: the Q4 plan now targets teams of 20–40 agents. Our interviews were with teams of 3–8.
7. Legal, about the workload PRD: agents' performance data (response times per person) can only go to HR with the agent's consent.
