# PRD: Rebalance the team's work

**Who:** team leads who watch the queue and hand out work.

**Problem:** when one agent is swamped and another has room, the lead only finds out when customers complain. Today a lead opens each agent's list one by one. The Q4 plan (Notion: "Helpline plan Q4 2026") says we're moving up to teams of 20–40 agents, where this gets much worse.

**Goal:** a lead can see who has too much and move requests to someone with room, in under a minute.

**Must support:**
- A "workload" view: each agent with their open and high-priority counts
- Moving several requests from one agent to another at once
- A weekly CSV for HR: each agent's handled requests and response times

**Nice to have:** suggest who should take a request, based on load.

**Not in scope:** changing how priority is set.
