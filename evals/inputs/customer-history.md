# PRD: Customer history

**Who:** support agents.

**Problem:** when a request comes in, agents can't see what the same customer asked before, so they ask again for details the customer already gave. Customers complain that they "have to repeat everything".

**Goal:** from a request, an agent can see the customer's earlier requests and how they were solved, without leaving the request.

**Must support:**
- The customer's past requests: subject, date, status, and who handled it
- Opening one to read it
- Customers with 50 or more past requests

**Nice to have:** notes about the customer that agents can add.

**Not in scope:** editing customer details.
