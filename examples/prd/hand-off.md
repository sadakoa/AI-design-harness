# Hand a request off to a teammate

**Who:** support agents, and team leads who rebalance work.

**Problem:** when an agent hands a request to someone else, the new assignee often doesn't know what's already been tried, so they ask the customer again.

**Goal:** a hand-off carries the context with it, and the agent who receives it can pick it up without rereading the whole conversation.

**Flow:** from the inbox, open a request, choose "Hand off", pick a teammate, write a short note, confirm, and land back in the inbox. The teammate sees the request at the top of their list with the note.

**Out of scope:** automatic routing rules.
